What Affects Price Most

Scope Complexity

Page count, content depth, integrations, and custom functionality.

Commercial Requirements

Lead generation vs ecommerce journeys need different architecture.

Delivery Model

Phased rollout usually reduces risk and improves budget efficiency.

How To Budget Better

1. Define business outcome first: leads, sales, bookings, or quote requests.
2. Split must-have vs later features: launch essentials in phase one.
3. Prioritise high-value pages: service pages, product pages, and conversion points.
4. Reserve optimisation budget: post-launch iteration is where ROI compounds.

Common Pricing Mistakes

Buying by Lowest Number

Low quotes often exclude critical conversion work, which then gets billed later as expensive “extras.”

Undefined Scope

If deliverables are vague, timeline and budget drift are almost guaranteed.

No Post-Launch Budget

Skipping optimisation budget usually stalls growth after launch, even if the site looks good.

FAQs

How much should I budget for a Birmingham website project?

Set budget based on commercial objective and scope. A lead-gen landing page and a full ecommerce build are fundamentally different projects.

Can we launch in phases?

Yes. Phased delivery is often the best way to reduce risk, manage cash flow, and get high-impact pages live first.

Yes. We can review scope quality, highlight hidden gaps, and suggest a more efficient rollout plan.
