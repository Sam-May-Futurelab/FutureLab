How To Budget Better

1. Define business outcome first: leads, sales, bookings, or quote requests.
2. Split must-have vs later features: launch essentials in phase one.
3. Prioritise high-value pages: service pages, product pages, and conversion points.
4. Reserve optimisation budget: post-launch iteration is where ROI compounds.
